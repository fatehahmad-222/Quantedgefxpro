Discipline, built in

The rules don't bend when the market gets loud

No martingale sizing

Losses aren't chased with larger position sizes. Every trade is sized from your risk profile, not from the outcome of the last one — the mechanism most EAs use to look good in a backtest and fail live.

Defined stop-loss

Every position opens with a stop-loss attached — never a trade running unprotected.

Session & news filters

Trading pauses around high-impact releases and thin-liquidity windows where slippage typically spikes.

No broker restriction

Runs on any MT5-enabled broker — there's no approved list and nothing to switch.

Full trade transparency

Every entry, exit, and adjustment is logged and exportable, so you can audit exactly what the system did and why — not just the headline result.

The guarantee

If our own settings blow your account, we refund the licence

Grid and martingale bots blow accounts because they multiply position size after a loss until one losing streak wipes the balance. Quant Edge FX Pro doesn't work that way.

  • —Takes roughly 2–3 trades a day, not dozens
  • —Never opens a new position while one is already open
  • —No lot-size multiplication after a loss — sizing stays fixed to your risk setting
  • —Runs 24 hours a day, 5 days a week, without a human at the screen

Licence-fee refund guarantee

If your account is blown while running Quant Edge FX Pro on our recommended 5% per-trade risk setting, on a supported MT5 broker, without manual interference or overridden lot sizing, we refund your licence fee.

This covers the cost of the software — it isn't compensation for trading losses. Trading gold on margin still carries risk; see the disclosure below.
